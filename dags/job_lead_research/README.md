# Job Lead Triage

Monitoring individual job boards to ensure rapid discovery while maintaining a high signal-to noise.

## Motivation

Aggregators like LinkedIn have lots of roles listed, but I have found they are largely stale or, even if they are relatively fresh, flooded with easy-apply submissions. As a result, I've been looking more to the individual companies' job boards, which are a better source of truth for active roles. This also allows me to vet the individual companies I want to keep an eye on instead of relying on the aggregators questionably accurate search funtionality, which is difficult to tune to the level of precision I want. In such an unstrcutured and self-driven search, I also needed something to prod me each morning to keep applying, and that handles the grunt work of sifting through a ton of roles that have no relevance to me anyway. Different firms classifying roles in different ways would make it increasingly difficult to mamually filter down listings to look at - for instance, should I look at the product department? IT? Engineering? Lastly, this system needed to ensure that the human stayed in the loop. This should not be a sloppy, spammy mass submission machine, but rather a tool that allows me to focus my time and effort on relevant listings.

## Objective

A system that:

- Discovers roles posted by companies within 24 hours of posting
- Identifies roles that are interesting and relevant for me
- Provides a daily digest of roles for me to decide on an apply to

Broadly, I want to wake up in the morning with a headstart and be able to make decisions, not be forced to slog through tedious research.
